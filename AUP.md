# TraceMark Acceptable Use Policy 可接受使用政策 利用規約

**English**

TraceMark generates **artistic, decorative** seal and postcard imagery. It is not a tool for producing documents with legal effect.

**Allowed:** personal names, given names, pen names, studio names (斋馆), auspicious phrases, place names, dates, monograms, floral/geometric motifs, and — in *postcard or commemorative-stamp style* — company, organization, government, and country names (e.g., a "visit Tokyo 2026" postcard).

**Not allowed:** anything intended to authenticate a document; seals replicating an existing organization's official seal for the purpose of signing or stamping official documents; counterfeit certificates, receipts, or contracts; impersonation of any real person's registered seal.

All outputs carry a permanent artification frame (perforation border or "TRACE·ART" microtext) and a non-standard artistic layout, making them visually and legally distinguishable from authentic seals. In China, criminal law (Article 280) protects seals *usable for authentication and signing*; decorative imagery without that capability falls outside its scope, and TraceMark's design guarantees that gap.

**日本語**

TraceMark は**装飾的なアートはんこ・飾りはんこ**を生成します。**実印・銀行印・認印などの法的効力を持つ印鑑ではありません。**会社の名前や公共機関の名前は「印章」モードでは使えません（切手・はがきスタイルでは使えます）。生成物には常に「TRACE・ART」のマイクロテキストとアート化枠が入り、本物の印鑑とは明らかに区別できます。

**简体中文**

TraceMark 生成的是**装饰性艺术印章与明信片**图像，不具备任何法律效力。印章模式下不接受公司、机构、政府名称（此类内容请切换到纪念邮票或明信片样式）。所有成品永久带有齿孔边框与 "TRACE·ART" 微字，并与真实印鉴在排版与细节上保持可感知差异，确保不可用于签署与认证场景。
